# Field profile

The reader is a mechanical engineering PhD candidate in computational materials science. Their work covers machine-learning interatomic potentials, DFT and molecular dynamics, atomistic foundation models, and high-performance computing.

## Core (always save)

- Machine-learning interatomic potentials: MACE, UMA, NequIP, Allegro, DeepMD, fine-tuning, LoRA, uncertainty.
- Atomistic foundation models and datasets: FAIR Chemistry, OMat, OMol, OC20/OC25, MatterGen, universal potentials, benchmarks.
- DFT and molecular-dynamics workflows, high-throughput simulation, active learning, enhanced sampling, free-energy methods.
- Generative and AI-for-chemistry/materials methods: Boltzmann generators, diffusion or flow models for molecules and crystals, inverse design.
- Molten salts, 2D materials and MoS2/TMD electronics, tribology (friction, wear, lubricants), electrochemical interfaces.

## Adjacent (save, lower priority)

- Scientific machine learning: neural operators, reduced-order models, physics-informed ML.
- AI agents for science, self-driving labs, AI coding agents studied scientifically.
- AI accelerators for scientific computing.
